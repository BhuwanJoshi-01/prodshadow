# Code of conduct

ProdShadow is maintained by people who want migrations to stop taking
production down. Everyone is welcome to help, and everyone is expected to
make that pleasant.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and assume good intent. Explain, don't belittle.
* Critique code and ideas, not people.
* Accept that "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are normal parts of
  engineering.
* Respect people's time: provide reproductions, read before asking, and
  follow up on what you start.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or
imagery, doxxing, sustained disruption, and encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on
the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors who
violate this code.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
